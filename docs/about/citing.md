# Citing and authors

## Authors

- **Anas Makdesi**: implementation and maintenance.
- **Antoine Girard** (Université Paris-Saclay, CNRS, CentraleSupélec, L2S) and **Laurent
  Fribourg** (Université Paris-Saclay, CNRS, ENS Paris-Saclay, LMF): co-authors of the methods
  implemented here (L4DC 2021, ADHS 2021, TAC 2024) and supervisors of the PhD thesis.
- **Majid Zamani** (University of Colorado Boulder; LMU Munich) and **Saber Jafarpour**
  (University of Colorado Boulder): co-authors of the safe-envelope methods
  ({mod}`ddma.invariance`, IFAC World Congress 2026).

## Citing

Please cite the TAC paper; for the bounded-derivative models also the thesis, and for the safe
envelopes the IFAC 2026 papers (Makdesi & Zamani, *Data-driven synthesis of controlled
invariant sets for monotone systems*; Makdesi, Zamani & Jafarpour, *Data-driven controlled
invariance via monotone embeddings*).
